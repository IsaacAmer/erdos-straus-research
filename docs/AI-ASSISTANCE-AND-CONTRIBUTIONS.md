# AI assistance and contribution disclosure

Use this as a transparent starting point; edit it so every sentence accurately describes your own work.

> AI tools (including ChatGPT and Google Gemini) were used as assistants during parts of the project for code-drafting suggestions, debugging, exploratory algebra, organization of research notes, and language editing. These tools were not treated as authoritative mathematical sources. Mathematical statements attributed to published work are cited to their sources; project-specific derivations are labeled as provisional where independent verification has not been obtained. The maintainer ran the listed commands locally, reviewed the program outputs, maintained the repository, and is responsible for the claims and limitations presented in the project. The repository does not claim a proof of the Erdős–Straus conjecture or established priority for the Phase 14 corollary.

## Keep an internal contribution record

| Task | Human contribution | AI assistance | Verification evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research question and scope | Fill in what you chose and why | Idea discussion | Dated research log |
| Code and tests | List modules you wrote/edited and ran | Suggestions/debugging | Git commits, test logs |
| Mathematical derivations | State what you can independently explain | Exploratory algebra/formatting | Proof notes and cited sources |
| Literature review | List papers you personally read | Search/summary assistance | Source bibliography and checked passages |
| Final report | Final wording and approval | Editing support | Tagged release |

Do not state that every line of code was written by you if AI generated parts. Do not imply that tests verify a proof. Do not list an AI system as a human author or recommender.
